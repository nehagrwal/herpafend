Herpafend Side Effects & Risks

The side effects of Herpafend, if any, depend on the ingredients, the dosage, the quality of the manufacturing process and the health of the person taking it. Taking zinc supplements on an empty stomach can lead to nausea, stomach upset and other digestive problems. High zinc intake over a long period of time can inhibit copper absorption, and cause anaemias or neurologic problems. The tolerable upper intake level for zinc for U.S. adults is 40 milligrammes a day from all sources unless your health care provider tells you otherwise.

Taking high doses of vitamin C can lead to diarrhoea, nausea or stomach cramps and too much vitamin D can cause high levels of calcium in the blood, leading to complications. The adult upper intake level for vitamin D is generally 4,000 international units per day from all sources, but it may vary with medically supervised treatment. These limits are not recommended targets. One should check the ingredient quantities of a product prior to use.
